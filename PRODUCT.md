# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Budget-$0 developers: student developers building portfolios, indie hackers running side projects on free-tier infrastructure, and full-stack engineers monitoring a side hustle. They land here because their uptime monitor of choice locks security auditing behind a paid tier, and they're in the app to check on real infrastructure, not to browse a marketing site. The primary surface is the authenticated dashboard — they're managing monitors, reading grades, and reacting to incidents, not shopping for a product.

## Product Purpose

KindleKeep bundles security auditing (HTTP header grading, TLS/certificate inspection, SSL expiry alerting) into free-tier uptime monitoring — the one thing competitors like UptimeRobot gate behind payment. Success looks like a user trusting the A–F grade enough to treat it as real signal, and coming back because the dashboard tells them something a plain uptime ping never would.

## Positioning

A $0 budget should not mean a $0 security posture — real security auditing, free, alongside the uptime monitoring everyone already expects.

## Brand Personality

Crisp, minimal, and confident — precise rather than decorative. The structure matches linear.app's product conventions (soft rounded corners, Mercury White / Nordic Gray neutrals, self-hosted Inter Variable typeface, hairline-first elevation), but the brand color is kindlekeep's own: **Signal Blue (#3B82F6)**, matched pixel-for-pixel to the product logo, not Linear's. Signal Blue is a deliberate, felt identity color (not a token accent buried in hover states), carried with the same restraint and confidence as Linear's interface: dense information presented calmly on a white surface, soft rounded structure, no wasted ornament.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-dashboard flatness: a "correct" but invisible accent color that technically exists in the CSS but reads as absent at a glance, gray-on-gray monotony, ungrounded pastel/cream neutrals, decorative gradients or glassmorphism with no purpose, side-stripe colored borders as a stand-in for real design decisions.

## Design Principles

Signal Blue means something and shows up where it's earned — primary actions, active state, key signal — not scattered as decoration. Dense, task-first layouts over spacious marketing-style whitespace; this is a tool, not a pitch. Soft rounded corners (Linear-style, not pill-shaped) and hairline structure over sharp edges or heavy shadows. Every screen should read as deliberately composed, not templated. Motion explains a state change; it never exists for its own sake — no idle-state decorative animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum across all text and UI components, including on the newly-committed blue surfaces. No color-only status indicators — pair every grade/status color with an icon or label.
